Terms and Conditions

Last updated 1 November 2026.

The rules for using the Mewvo Discord bot, this website and its plans. By using any of them you accept these terms. If you don’t, please don’t use them.

Who runs Mewvo

Mewvo is run by one private individual, its owner. Contact: ask in the support server. Mewvo is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Discord.

Who can use it

You must be allowed to use Discord, which means at least 13 years old, or older where the law of your country says so, and you must follow Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Using the bot

  • Follow the rules of the server you are in.
  • Don’t use Mewvo to harass anyone, to spam, to share illegal content or to get around a punishment.
  • Don’t try to break, overload, reverse engineer or misuse the bot, this website or the dashboard, and don’t look for or use security holes. If you find one, please report it privately.

Moderation decisions

Mewvo carries out what a server’s staff decide. Warnings, mutes, kicks and bans are decisions of that server’s staff team; automod and the warning ladder follow the rules that server has set. The owner of Mewvo is not responsible for decisions a server’s staff make with it.

Appeals

If a punishment can be appealed, the DM about it says how. One appeal per case can be open at a time, and staff decide and tell you the result. Some punishments cannot be appealed; the DM says so.

What you post

What you write stays yours. You allow Mewvo to store and show it only as far as needed to run the feature you used, for example to show your appeal to staff or your suggestion in the suggestions channel.

Plans and keys

  • No payments are taken yet. The checkout on this website is a test: no money is charged and no payment details are asked for. A test plan can be ended or removed at any time.
  • A plan belongs to your Discord account and lasts for the time shown at checkout. It does not renew by itself.
  • Higher limits for a server (tags, sticky messages, feeds, scheduled messages) come from the plan of that server’s owner.
  • Buying or redeeming a higher plan while one is running converts the time left into the new plan, by price. A lower plan cannot be added while a higher one runs.
  • A key can be redeemed once, by one account. Gift keys must be redeemed within one year; other keys show their own date, if any. Keys cannot be exchanged for money.
  • Keys obtained by fraud or abuse, or plans used to break these terms, can be revoked.
  • Features marked “Planned” are not built yet and are not part of what a plan gives you today.

Before real payments start, these terms will be updated with the price, taxes, payment, renewal, cancellation and your right of withdrawal, and you will be asked to accept them at checkout. Nothing changes for a test plan you already have.
