About Mewvo

Mewvo is the bot of the Mewvo Discord server, built and run by its owner for that community.

How it started

Mewvo began as a fork of TitanBot, an open-source (MIT) Discord bot, and has since been rebuilt around the server’s own needs: an enforcement system with case codes and appeals, a warning ladder, automod, tickets and modmail, and a web dashboard for the staff team.

How it treats members

  • Every punishment comes with a DM that says what happened, why, until when, and whether and how it can be appealed.
  • Staff actions are recorded as cases, so decisions can be reviewed and corrected.
  • Verification asks Discord only for your identity and permission to add you to the support server, never your email.
  • Members can delete their own data from the bot in a server with /wipedata.

Availability

For now Mewvo runs only on its own server; it can’t be added to other servers yet.

Get help

Questions, appeals or problems: join the support server.
