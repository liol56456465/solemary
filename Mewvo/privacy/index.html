Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

What Mewvo stores about you, why, for how long, who else sees it, and how to get a copy or have it deleted. Your data is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models.

Who is responsible

Mewvo is run by one private individual, its owner, who decides how the data below is used (the “controller” under the GDPR). To reach them about your data, ask in the support server.

In short

  • Mewvo works with Discord IDs (the numbers that identify accounts, servers and channels). Where a record needs a readable name, such as a moderation case, the username at that moment is saved with it.
  • Everything is kept in a PostgreSQL database used only by the bot.
  • The bot never asks for your email address, a password or payment details. Only if you choose to create a Mewvo account on this website, Mewvo stores your email address and a one-way hash of your password (see below).
  • This website has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party scripts. It sets cookies only to keep you signed in.

If a server turns on logging, events such as joins, leaves, role changes and edited or deleted messages (including their text) are posted to that server’s staff log channels on Discord. Those posts live on Discord, not in Mewvo’s database.

Why this is allowed (legal bases)

  • Providing what you use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): leveling, tickets, modmail, things you save, plans and keys, and your Mewvo account.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): keeping servers safe, enforcing their rules, preventing ban evasion and abuse, and keeping the bot running. You can object to this; see your rights below.
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): for example, keeping invoices once real payments start.

Automatic decisions

Automod can warn, mute or kick automatically when a message breaks a rule the server has turned on, and the warning ladder bans automatically after the third warning. Every automatic action becomes a normal case with a card in the staff channel, where a person can keep, undo or change it, and you can appeal it the same way as any other case.

Who else sees your data

  • Discord, which the bot runs on. Discord’s own privacy policy covers what it does with your data.
  • The hosting provider that runs the bot and the database provider that stores its data. They process it only to provide their service.
  • The email provider (Resend) that delivers the emails for a Mewvo account (confirm your email, reset your password). It gets your email address and the text of the email, only to deliver it.
  • The staff of the server you are in see the moderation, appeal, ticket and modmail records of that server.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU and EEA. Where they do, they rely on the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Nothing is sold or given to anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Cookies

The website only sets cookies that are needed to sign in and stay signed in: a session cookie, and short-lived cookies that protect the sign-in forms, the Discord sign-in step and the two-step login step. They are not used for tracking and are deleted when you sign out or when they expire. Because they are strictly necessary, no cookie banner is needed. Your theme choice in the dashboard is a cookie too.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for:

  • a copy of the data Mewvo has about you (access, Art. 15), also in a machine-readable form (portability, Art. 20);
  • wrong data to be corrected (Art. 16);
  • your data to be deleted (Art. 17), or its use to be limited (Art. 18);
  • your data to no longer be used for a legitimate interest (objection, Art. 21).

To use any of these, ask in the support server. You get an answer within one month. Some safety records, such as an active ban, may be kept for as long as they are still needed to keep a server safe; if so, you are told why.

If you have a Mewvo account, you can delete it yourself in Account settings on the website (your password is needed). This deletes the username, email, password hash and two-step login at once. A plan and the early-account bonus record stay with your Discord account; ask if you want those deleted too.

You can also delete some data yourself: run /wipedata in a server to delete your levels and XP, your economy balance, your AFK status and your birthday in that server, and your personal to-do list and reminders. It does not delete moderation records, appeals, reports or verification records.

If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority of the EU country where you live or work, or where the problem happened.

Children

Mewvo is meant for people old enough to use Discord: at least 13, or older where the law of your country says so. To create a Mewvo account you enter your date of birth; it is only used to check that you are 13 or older and is not saved. It does not knowingly collect data about anyone younger. If you think it has, ask in the support server and it will be deleted.

Security

Sign-in tokens are encrypted, IP addresses are only stored as keyed hashes, session IDs are only stored as hashes, passwords only as scrypt hashes, two-step login secrets only encrypted, email links and recovery codes only as hashes, the database connection uses TLS, and staff permissions are checked again on every action.

Changes

When what Mewvo stores changes, this page is updated along with the date at the top. Bigger changes are also announced in the support server.
