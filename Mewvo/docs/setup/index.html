Set up Mewvo in 10 minutes

For the server owner: the few settings Mewvo needs, in order, on the dashboard.

You need the Administrator permission in the server. Everything below is done on the dashboard, in your browser. You do not need to type any commands.

1. Open the dashboard

Go to the dashboard and log in with Discord. Mewvo only asks Discord who you are. It does not see your email or your messages.

2. Follow "Setup step by step"

On the Overview page, press Set up Mewvo step by step. It shows one setting at a time, says why it matters, and opens the right page. Skip anything you do not need. The most important three are:

  1. Mod log channel: a staff-only channel where Mewvo writes down every warning, mute, kick and ban.
  2. Muted role: Mewvo makes it by itself the first time someone is muted, so you can leave it.
  3. Appeals category: where Mewvo opens a private channel when a member asks to have a punishment lifted.

3. Write your rules

On the Rules page, add your server rules. They show on a web page, and every punishment message names the rule that was broken.

4. Check automod

On the Automod page you choose what automod does. If you are not sure yet, pick Watch only: automod then only tells staff what it would have done, and nothing happens to anyone until a staff member agrees. Switch to Act when you trust it.

5. Try it on yourself

In Discord, run /test. Mewvo sends you the exact message a member would get for a warning or a ban. Nothing is done to anyone.

Next

The sidebar shows the main pages. Press Show all pages at the bottom to see everything. If a word is new to you, open Words explained in the sidebar. Questions? Ask in the support server.
