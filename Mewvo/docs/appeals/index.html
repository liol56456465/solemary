Appeals: how they work

Where to appeal, what the form asks, who decides and how long it takes to try again.

Where to appeal

Every punishment DM has the appeal link, or says it can’t be appealed. The appeal panel has three buttons: Appeal, My appeals and My standing. Muted or warned members use the panel in the main server. Banned members use the one in the support server, which they can still reach after a ban.

The form

Why should it be lifted?
At least 20 characters, at most 1000.
Case code
From the DM, for example k7xq2.
More info
Optional, anything else staff should know.

What happens next

  • Ban and kick appeals open a channel in the support server with you in it, so staff can ask questions.
  • Mute and warning appeals are reviewed by staff in the main server.
  • The administrators decide. You get the decision by DM. An accepted appeal lifts what is still active and, for bans and kicks, comes with a one-time invite back.

Limits

  • One pending appeal per case.
  • After a denial, wait 7 days before appealing the same case again.
  • Bans and mutes can be appealed while they last. Warnings and kicks within 30 days.
  • Some cases can’t be appealed, for example a permanent ladder ban after 6 months. The DM says so.
