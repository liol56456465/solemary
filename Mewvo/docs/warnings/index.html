How warnings and bans escalate

The warning ladder: three warnings, then 7 days, 30 days and a permanent ban.

Every member has a ladder in each server. It starts at level 0 with no warnings, and it never resets on its own.

The steps

  1. Warnings 1 and 2 are a DM: “Warning 1 of 3” and “Warning 2 of 3”.
  2. Warning 3 is an automatic 7-day ban. The member is now at level 1.
  3. After they return, the next warning is an automatic 30-day ban (level 2).
  4. The warning after that is a permanent ban (level 3). It can be appealed for 6 months. After that, or after a denied appeal, it can no longer be appealed.

What changes the ladder

Remove warn
Lowers the warning count.
Accepted appeal of a ladder ban
Lowers the level by one, never below 0.
A ban given by hand
Does not move the ladder.
Voiding a case
The case stays on record but no longer counts.
Imported old warnings
Shown as history only. They don’t count.

Members can check where they are with My standing on the appeal panel.
