A Discord bot for moderation and appeals

Mewvo warns, mutes, kicks and bans from one panel. The member gets a DM with the reason, the end time and how to appeal. Your staff get a case with a short code they can look up later.

Mewvo runs on the Mewvo server for now. It can’t be added to other servers yet.

Everything in one place

Members, cases, appeals and every setting in the browser. Sign in with Discord; every change is in an audit log. A tour of the dashboard

The same rules for everyone

Warnings climb a fixed ladder per member and server. Two warnings are a DM. The third is a 7-day ban, then 30 days, then permanent. Manual bans don’t move it, and an accepted appeal moves it back down a step.

Members can check where they stand, so nobody is surprised by a ban. How warnings escalate

What it does

Moderation first, with the community tools a server needs next to it. Every feature

  • Moderation

    Warn, mute, kick and ban from one panel that shows the member’s history first. Every action is a case with a short code that staff can edit, void or add evidence to later.

    Getting started

  • Appeals

    Members appeal with a short form. Ban appeals open a channel in the support server, so they work even after a ban, and a person on the staff team decides.

    How appeals work

  • Automod

    Fourteen rules, from scam links to mass mentions. Each automatic action is a normal case, with a card in the staff channel to keep, undo or change it.

    Automod rules

  • Tickets and modmail

    A ticket panel, and members can DM the bot to reach staff. Both become channels in your server; lines starting with // stay internal.

    Tickets and modmail

  • Logging and tools

    Joins, leaves, role changes, edited and deleted messages and more, each in the channel you choose. Verification and server tools sit next to it.

    Logging and tools

  • Community tools

    Leveling, tags, suggestions, starboard, sticky messages, YouTube feeds, reaction roles, giveaways, birthdays, applications, join-to-create voice channels, welcome messages.

    All community tools
