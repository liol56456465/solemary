A Discord bot for moderation and appeals

Mewvo warns, mutes, kicks and bans from one panel. The member gets a DM with the reason, the end time and how to appeal, and your staff get a case they can review later.

Join the support serverRead the docsOpen the dashboard

Mewvo runs on the Mewvo server for now. It can’t be added to other servers yet.

What it does

Moderation
Warn, mute, kick and ban from one panel that shows the member’s history first. Every action is a case with a short code that staff can edit, void or add evidence to later. Getting started
Warning ladder
Warnings 1 and 2 are a DM. The third is a 7-day ban, then a 30-day ban, then a permanent one. It is the same for everyone and members can check where they stand. How warnings escalate
Appeals
Members appeal with a short form. Ban appeals open a channel in the support server, so they work even after a ban, and a person on the staff team decides. How appeals work
Automod
Fourteen rules, from scam links to mass mentions. Each automatic action is a normal case, with a card in the staff channel to keep, undo or change it. Automod rules
Tickets and modmail
A ticket panel, and members can DM the bot to reach staff. Both become channels in your server; lines starting with // stay internal. Tickets and modmail
Web dashboard
Members, cases, appeals and every setting in the browser. Sign in with Discord; every change is in an audit log. A tour of the dashboard
Community tools
Leveling, tags, suggestions, starboard, sticky messages, YouTube feeds, reaction roles, giveaways, birthdays, applications, join-to-create voice channels, welcome messages. All community tools

Price

Mewvo is free. Paid plans raise limits such as tags and sticky messages: Normal €1.99, Pro €4.99 and Max €9.99 a month. Plans and prices
