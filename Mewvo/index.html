Discord moderation with a paper trail.

Mewvo warns, mutes, kicks and bans from one panel. The member gets a DM with the reason, the end time and how to appeal. Your staff get a case they can review, correct or void.

01Enforcement

One panel, with the member’s history on top.

Run /enforcements on a member and Mewvo shows their account age, warnings, ladder level, active punishments and last cases before you choose what to do.

Action→Reason→Duration→Confirm

  • The DM goes out before a kick or ban, so it still reaches the member. If it fails, the moderator is told.
  • Every action is a case with a short code, and staff can edit it, void it or attach evidence later.
  • It refuses the server owner, the bot, yourself and anyone with an equal or higher role.
  • More than 5 bans or kicks in 10 minutes blocks that moderator for an hour and alerts the owner.

02Warning ladder

Three warnings, then a ban. No surprises.

The ladder counts per member and per server, and it never quietly resets. Each warning DM says which one it is. The third is an automatic 7-day ban. After that, the next warning is a 30-day ban, and the one after is permanent, still open to appeal for six months.

  • Bans a moderator gives by hand don’t move the ladder.
  • Removing a warning lowers the count again.
  • An accepted appeal of a ladder ban steps the level down by one.

How warnings escalate, step by step

03Appeals

Appeals reach a person, even after a ban.

Members press Appeal on the appeal panel and fill in three fields. Ban and kick appeals open a private channel in the support server, so the conversation works even when they can’t see your server any more. Staff see the case and the member’s history next to Accept and Deny.

  • One pending appeal per case. After a denial, the member waits 7 days to try again.
  • Bans and mutes can be appealed while they last; warnings and kicks within 30 days.
  • Accepting lifts the punishment and DMs a one-time invite back to the server.

How appeals work

04Automod

Automod that shows its work.

Fourteen rules, from scam links and mass mentions to caps and fancy fonts. Each rule has its own punishment steps, and every automatic action is a normal case with a review card in your staff channel: keep it, undo it or change it.

  • Text is folded back to plain letters first, so look-alike characters and zalgo don’t slip past.
  • Links, attachments and fast posting are limited for new members in their first 3 days.
  • Ten joins in a minute counts as a raid: the server locks down, invites and DMs pause, and admins are alerted.
  • Works next to Discord’s own AutoMod, with a bridge for its rules.

05Tickets and modmail

Tickets and modmail in channels your staff already use.

A ticket panel with claim, priority, saved answers and a transcript when the ticket closes. Members can also DM the bot and choose Send to staff: the conversation becomes a staff channel, and anything a moderator starts with // stays internal.

  • Close, block and unblock with /modmail.
  • Members can report a staff member privately; only administrators see those reports.

06Dashboard

The whole server in a browser tab.

Members, cases, appeals, the staff inbox and every module’s settings, on one page per area. Sign in with Discord. Administrator is checked again on every request, and every change is written to an audit log with the old and the new value.
