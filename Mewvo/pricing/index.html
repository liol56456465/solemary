Questions about pricing

More answers in the FAQ.

Can I buy a plan now?
No. Mewvo runs only on its own server for now. Paid plans start when it opens to other servers; until then nothing can be bought and nobody is charged.
What currency are prices in?
Euros. The prices on this page are the planned monthly and yearly prices.
Can I cancel?
Yes, any time. A paid plan is meant to run until the end of the period you paid for and then fall back to Free.
What does Free keep?
Everything Mewvo does today: enforcement, the warning ladder, appeals, automod, tickets, modmail, the dashboard and the community tools. Your case history is never deleted when a plan ends.
What happens to extra tags or feeds if I go back to Free?
The plan is that nothing is deleted: items over the Free limit stop working until you remove some or upgrade again. This may still change before launch.
